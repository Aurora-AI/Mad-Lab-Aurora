## 🏗️ Structural Integrity Audit

### 1. Test of Structural Collapse (16px)
- **Result**: **PASS**
- **Analysis**: The high-contrast binary (B&W) ensures that the massive typography and geometric grid remain legible even at extreme reductions. No gradients or shadows to "wash out" the structure.

### 2. Typographic Silence
- **Result**: **PASS**
- **Constraint**: Body text will act as signalization (Wayfinding). Support fonts must be robust but invisible in the hierarchy against the **17.36vw Headlines**.

### 3. Boolean Geometric Audit (The Monotrail)
- **Grid**: 12-Column Fluid.
- **Rules**: Zero rounded corners ($0px$). Mathematical precision on all margins ($8vw$ vertical breathing).
- **Veto**: Card layouts and SaaS standard noise are REJECTED.

## 🎭 Atmospheric Constraints (Stitch Parameters)

- **Palette**: `#000000` (Piano Black) / `#FFFFFF` (Absolute White).
- **Secondary Accent**: `#00FFEF` (Cyan/Aqua) exclusively for micro-states and "Integrity" indicators.
- **Letter Spacing**: `-0.8vw` (Cinematic Weight).

## 🚀 Deterministic Prompt (For Phase 2)

> *"Generate a hyper-premium, high-contrast B&W editorial landing page for Elysian Aurora. Visual Rules: Massive headlines at 17.36vw with -0.8vw tracking. Fluid 12-column grid system with 8vw vertical padding between blocks. Architecture: Monotrail (linear narrativa, no top menu). Footer to invert B&W contrast. No noise, no shadows, no rounding."*
